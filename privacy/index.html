Privacy policy

Effective date: 4 November 2026

This privacy policy explains which data is processed when you visit this website and when you use the app PilotsWeather for iPhone, iPad and Android.

1. Controller

Responsible for data processing is the provider named in the imprint. You can reach us at pilotsweather@codingpilot.de.

2. This website

Hosting and server logs

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you open a page, your browser transmits technical data such as your IP address, the date and time, the page requested and your browser type to GitHub's servers. GitHub needs this data to deliver the website and to protect it against abuse, and may store it in server logs. We have no access to these logs. Legal basis is our legitimate interest in a secure and reliable website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Data may be transferred to the USA; GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Details: GitHub Privacy Statement.

No cookies, no analytics, no third-party content

The website sets no cookies, uses no analytics or tracking tools and loads no content from third parties. Fonts are served from this website itself.

Email

If you write to us by email, we use your email address and message only to answer your request (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR) and delete them when they are no longer needed.

3. The app PilotsWeather

No account

PilotsWeather doesn't require an account. We don't ask for your name, address or email address.

Weather data

To load METAR and TAF, the app requests the reports for your airports from aviationweather.gov (Aviation Weather Center of the US National Weather Service). As with any internet request, your IP address is visible to that server.

Your airports and settings

The airports you add and your settings are stored on your device.

Location

If you allow it, the app uses your location only to find the nearest stations. Your coordinates are processed on the device and are not sent to us.

Calendar Connect

If you allow calendar access, the app reads today's events from the calendar you choose to find airport codes in their titles. Calendar events are processed only on your device and are never uploaded. You can revoke calendar access at any time in the system settings.

Feedback by email

If you send feedback from the app, your email app opens with a prepared message. We use what you send only to answer you and to fix the problem you describe.

Purchases

Premium is bought through the App Store or Google Play; Apple or Google process the payment. We don't receive or store any payment data. To unlock Premium, the app checks your purchase with RevenueCat (RevenueCat Inc., USA), which receives an anonymous app user ID and the purchase receipt from the store. Legal basis is the performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Anonymous usage statistics and error reports

To improve PilotsWeather, the app sends anonymous usage statistics to Aptabase (Technov Solutions SRL, Romania; processed and stored exclusively in the EU). They include which features are used (for example opening the calendar or the Premium page), the airports (ICAO codes) you add or remove, the app version, operating system and version, device model, language setting, and the country or region derived from your IP address. Your IP address itself is not stored.

When the app runs into an error or crashes, it also sends an error report to Aptabase: the type of error, its technical message and where in the app's code it happened, together with the app version, operating system and device model. Error reports don't contain your name or contact details; in rare cases the technical message can include details of the failed request, such as an airport code. Events and reports are stored on the device until they are sent, so a crash report is sent the next time you start the app.

We don't use cookies, device identifiers or advertising identifiers. The data can't be linked to you, to other apps or across days, and it is neither sold nor used for advertising. Legal basis is our legitimate interest in improving the app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The data is kept for up to 5 years.

You can switch this off at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy → “Share anonymous usage statistics and error reports”. After that, no usage statistics and no error reports are sent.

4. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure and restriction of processing of your personal data, the right to data portability and the right to object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 15–21 GDPR). To exercise these rights, write to pilotsweather@codingpilot.de. Since we don't store data that identifies you, we may not be able to assign data to you.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

5. Changes

We update this privacy policy when the website or the app changes. The current version is always available on this page.
