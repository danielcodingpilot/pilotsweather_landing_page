2. What the app does

PilotsWeather shows published aviation weather reports (METAR and TAF) and information derived from them, such as flight categories, a TAF timeline and runway wind components.

All data in the app is for information only. PilotsWeather is not an official source of aviation weather and does not replace the weather briefing and flight preparation required by law. Reports may be delayed, incomplete or wrong, and derived values (for example flight categories or wind components) are calculated automatically and may differ from official evaluations. Decisions about a flight remain the sole responsibility of the pilot in command.
