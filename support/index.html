Stations

How do I add an airport?

Tap + on the stations screen and search by ICAO code or name. Stations near you are listed first if you allow location access.

An airport shows no METAR or TAF

Not every airport publishes a METAR, and many smaller airports have no TAF. If a report is missing for a while, the station may not have reported recently; the app shows how old each report is.

Flight categories

PilotsWeather uses the FAA flight categories, based on ceiling and visibility from the METAR or TAF:

  • VFR: ceiling above 3,000 ft and visibility above 5 SM (8,000 m)
  • MVFR: ceiling 1,000–3,000 ft or visibility 3–5 SM (4,800–8,000 m)
  • IFR: ceiling 500 ft to below 1,000 ft or visibility 1 SM to below 3 SM (1,600–4,800 m)
  • LIFR: ceiling below 500 ft or visibility below 1 SM (1,600 m)

The lower of the two values decides. The categories are a quick orientation, not a legal VFR minimum.

Calendar Connect

My airports from the calendar don't show up

  • Check that calendar access is allowed in the system settings.
  • Check in the app's settings that the right calendar is selected.
  • The airport's ICAO or IATA code (e.g. EDDF or FRA) must be in the title of an event today.

Without Premium, the weather is shown in full for the first airport in your calendar.

Premium

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Open the Premium page in the app and tap “Restore Purchase”, signed in with the same Apple or Google account.

How do I cancel?

In the subscription settings of your Apple ID (iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions) or in Google Play (Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions), at the latest 24 hours before the end of the period.

Privacy

How do I switch off usage statistics?

In the app's settings under Privacy. After that, no usage statistics and no error reports are sent.

How do I delete my data?

Your airports and settings are stored only on your device; deleting the app removes them. The anonymous statistics can't be linked to you. Details are in the privacy policy.

System requirements

iPhone and iPad with iOS 15 or later, Android 6.0 or later. Languages: English and German.
