Features

Everything for the weather brief. Nothing more.

Built by a pilot for the quick check before a flight: the raw reports you know, made easy to read.

Decoded METAR

Wind, visibility, clouds, temperature, dew point and QNH at a glance, with the raw report one tap away to copy.

Flight categories

VFR, MVFR, IFR and LIFR colour-coded for every station, so a glance at your list shows where it’s getting tight.

TAF timeline

One bar shows the flight category across the whole TAF period. Tap a section to see which conditions apply when.

Runway wind

Head- or tailwind and crosswind for every runway from the current METAR, with the best runway highlighted.

Your flights from the calendar

Put EDDF or FRA in a calendar event and PilotsWeather shows today’s weather for it automatically.

METAR history

See how the weather developed at a station over the last hours: is the fog lifting or the ceiling coming down?

Your stations

All your airports on one screen.

Add your home base, alternates and destinations by ICAO code, name or the nearest station. The list shows the flight category and the latest METAR for each, refreshed with a pull.

Airport page

METAR, TAF and history together.

The airport page decodes the METAR, highlights the TAF changes that matter and keeps the last reports below, so trends are obvious without reading every line.

METAR history · Premium

See the fog coming. And going.

The last 24 hours of METARs with their flight category show at a glance how the weather developed: when the fog came in, how low it got and when it lifted.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I use PilotsWeather for my official pre-flight briefing?

No. PilotsWeather is a quick overview and doesn’t replace the official weather briefing required for your flight. Always check the official sources before you fly.

Where does the weather data come from?

METAR and TAF come from the Aviation Weather Center of the US National Weather Service (aviationweather.gov), which publishes reports from airports worldwide. Runway data comes from OurAirports.

Which airports are available?

Every airport worldwide that publishes a METAR or TAF. Search by ICAO code or name, or let the app find the nearest station.

How does Calendar Connect find my airports?

Choose a calendar in the settings and write the airport’s ICAO or IATA code (e.g. EDDF or FRA) in the title of the event. PilotsWeather shows the weather for the airports in today’s events. The calendar is read only on your phone.

Does the app work offline?

Reports you have already loaded stay visible without a connection, and the app shows that it is offline. For current weather you need an internet connection.

What does Premium cost and how do I cancel?

Premium is available monthly or yearly; the app shows the current price from the App Store or Google Play. You cancel in the subscription settings of your Apple or Google account, at the latest 24 hours before the end of the period.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 15 or later, and Android phones with Android 6.0 or later. The app is available in English and German and supports dark mode.
